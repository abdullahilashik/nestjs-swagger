### 2. General Instructions for Testing

1. **Log in:** Open the web application URL, enter the provided test credentials, and log into the Dealercore dashboard.
2. **Facebook / Meta Integration Setup:** Navigate to **Settings $\rightarrow$ Integrations** and click **Connect Meta Account**.
3. **Facebook Login for Business Flow:** In the Facebook Login popup window, log in using the assigned Meta test user account (or accept the permissions prompt) to grant access to Business Manager, Pages, Instagram accounts, and Ad Accounts.
4. **Testing Content Publishing (`pages_manage_posts`, `instagram_content_publish`):**
* Navigate to **Content Planner / Post Composer**.
* Select a connected Facebook Page or Instagram Business Profile.
* Upload an image or video asset, compose a caption, and click **Publish Now**.
* Verify post publication confirmation on screen.


5. **Testing Page & Comment Engagement (`pages_manage_engagement`, `instagram_manage_comments`):**
* Navigate to the **Inbox / Social Moderation** tab.
* View live comments pulled from connected Facebook Pages / Instagram Business accounts.
* Click on a comment thread and post a test reply or perform a moderation action (like/hide comment).


6. **Testing Marketing API & Ads (`ads_read`, `ads_management`):**
* Navigate to **Marketing Analytics / Campaign Manager**.
* View ad account performance metrics (impressions, spend, clicks) loaded via the Ads Insights API.
* Test modifying an active campaign status (Pause/Active) or updating daily budgets.
